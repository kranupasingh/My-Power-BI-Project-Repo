# Power BI Retail & Sales Analytics Project

## 📊 Overview
This project was completed during the **Data Technician Bootcamp** and focuses on building interactive and insightful Power BI reports. The goal was to transform raw retail and sales data into meaningful dashboards that highlight trends, performance, and profitability through effective data storytelling.

## 🛠 Skills Demonstrated
- **Data Transformation & Cleaning**  
  - Used **Power Query** to import, clean, and shape datasets.  
  - Applied transformations such as filtering, renaming, and creating relationships between tables.

- **Interactive Reporting**  
  - Built reports with **slicers and filters** to allow dynamic exploration of data.  
  - Designed dashboards that enable users to drill down into categories, time periods, and regions.

- **DAX Calculations**  
  - Created **calculated columns** and **measures** using DAX (Data Analysis Expressions).  
  - Examples include profit margins, year-over-year growth, and cumulative sales.

- **Data Visualization**  
  - Developed a variety of visuals to tell the retail and sales story:
    - 📊 **Bar Charts** – Category vs. Sales, stacked bar with data labels.  
    - 📈 **Line Charts** – Sales and Profit trends over time.  
    - 🧾 **Card Visuals** – Key metrics like total sales and profit.  
    - 🥧 **Pie Charts** – Distribution of sales by product category.  
    - 🗺 **Map Visuals** – Geographic breakdown of sales performance.

## 🎯 Key Insights
- Identified top-performing product categories and regions.  
- Visualised profitability trends alongside sales growth.  
- Highlighted areas of decline and opportunities for improvement.  
- Demonstrated how interactive dashboards can support **data-driven decision-making**.

## 🚀 Publishing & Sharing
- Reports were published to the **Power BI Service** for online access.  
- Shared dashboards with peers to encourage collaboration and feedback.  
- Built a foundation for a **data portfolio** showcasing analytics skills.

## 📚 Learning Outcomes
- Gained hands-on experience with **Power BI Desktop & Service**.  
- Strengthened understanding of **data storytelling** through visuals.  
- Practiced industry-relevant skills in **data cleaning, modeling, and visualization**.  

---

✨ This project demonstrates how raw retail and sales data can be transformed into actionable insights using Power BI. It highlights the importance of combining technical skills with storytelling to make data accessible and impactful.
